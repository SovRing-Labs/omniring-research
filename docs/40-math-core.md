## What it is
Ten math concepts made executable and checkable: CRT reconstruction, mixed-radix conversion, Barrett reduction, Hamming distance, integer
square root by Newton iteration, period mining, absmean ternary quantisation, numerically stable softmax, Fibonacci / golden-ratio recurrence,
and the residue clock read. Each passes five stages:
1. **Spec** — a name-free equation derived by the doctor's black-box station (doc 37) from a reference driver written from the published
   definition, checked on every row; 10 rows passed the clean-room gate, none refused.
2. **Laws** — two to four properties per concept (never the definition restated), cemented by executed tests, each with a teeth check: break
   the subject on purpose and the law must fail.
3. **Emit** — the doctor generates Rust from the equation and verifies it on the spec rows.
4. **Generators** — per concept a maker (levels 1–5), a solver with a trace and an independent verifier using a different method.
5. **Explain** — given rows, run every type-compatible concept's verified program and report every concept that agrees on all rows, else `none`.

harness and result paths in this repository. "Section n" and "§n" inside it refer to the sections of that chapter; the matching documents here
are listed in the README. "Recorded; no saved result" marks a figure the project recorded whose output file is not published.*

## Math core: ten concepts as specs, laws, emitted code, generators and an explainer

- **What it is.** A knowledge base of ten math concepts made executable and checkable: CRT reconstruction (`crt2`), mixed-radix conversion (`mixed_radix`), Barrett reduction (`mod_barrett`), Hamming distance (`hamming`), integer square root by Newton iteration (`isqrt`), period mining (`period`), absmean ternary quantisation (`absmean_quant`), numerically stable softmax (`softmax`), Fibonacci, the golden-ratio recurrence (`fib`), residue clock read (`clock_read`). One planned concept (Plotkin anti-unification) was replaced by `isqrt` because the evaluator had no tree values yet. Pipeline: **Spec stage** - a name-free equation (an S-expression over `fold`, `range`, `sel`, arithmetic) derived by the doctor's black-box station from a reference driver written from the published definition (`harness/part7/ch5-math/m1/ref.py`), checked on every row, stored with 8 examples, 12 held-out rows and the reader's provenance (`docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/m1/specs.jsonl`, 10 rows; the wall's output `docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/m1/specs.wall.jsonl` has 10 rows and `docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/m1/refused.jsonl` has none). **Laws stage** - 2 to 4 properties per concept (never the definition restated), cemented by executed tests; each law has a teeth check: break the subject on purpose and the law must fail. **Emit stage** - the doctor's `need --emit` generates Rust from the equation and verifies it against the spec rows (`docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/emit/*.rs`, `docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/emit/index.json`). **Generator stage** - per concept a maker (levels 1 to 5), a solver with trace and an independent verifier that uses a different method than the solver (`harness/part7/ch5-math/gen/mathgen.py`). **Explain stage** - `math-kb explain --rows F` (the `math-kb` program): for every type-compatible concept, compile its verified emitted program and run it on the rows; report every concept that agrees on all rows, else `none`.
- **Contract.** Registry core `math.kb` (kind cli, version label `math-kb-0`), ops `explain`, `check` (re-run every law on a scratch ledger with a fresh seed, plus every generator's mutation check), `emit`, `gen`, and `list`; gate `math-kb check --n 200` (`docs/appendix/part7/ch5-registry/results/cores-registry-summary.json`); receipts as section 1 (with the input-hash exception noted there). The receipts file holds 18 `math.kb` receipts of 2026-10-08 (all in `docs/appendix/part7/ch5-registry/results/receipts-sample.jsonl`): 14 `explain` fires (the ten concepts each named by exactly the right concept, then four `none`), three `check` fires with `laws_passing 10/10`, `generators_clean true` at three different seeds, and one gate fire with the same summary.
- **Gate G1 / emit stage (harness: doctor `need --emit`; result `docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/m3.impl.jsonl`, `docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/m3.impl.log`): 10/10 emitted programs verified**, rows checked per concept from 1,207 (`isqrt`) to 2,084 (crt2 1,369; mod_barrett 1,998; mixed_radix 2,082; the other five 2,084). PARTIAL: the doctor binary is not in this repository, so the run is not reproducible here; the result files are.
- **Gate G2 / explain stage (harness `harness/part7/ch5-math/m5/explain_test.py` with the `math-kb` program; result `docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/m5/result.json`; inputs `docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/m5/rows.*.jsonl`, `docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/m5/neg.*.jsonl`).** Bar: explain names the concept from fresh rows for at least 8 of 10. Fresh rows: 24 per concept from the generators at levels 1 to 3, seed 99. **Result: 10/10 named exactly** (concepts with the same argument types are told apart by behaviour), and the **4 negative controls** (`a+b`, `n^2+1`, list length, reversed floats; 24 rows each) **all return `none`**. **Re-run** read-only in a scratch copy (the harness's fixed workspace path replaced, `rustc` available): the same 10/10 and 4/4, a byte-identical `result.json` and byte-identical row files. SUPPORTED. Limits: rows come from this project's own generators; four negatives; ten concepts out of a 145-concept index.
- **Generator mutation (generator stage; harness `harness/part7/ch5-math/gen/mathgen.py mutate --n 200`).** Re-run read-only in a scratch copy (deterministic: two runs give the same output): right answers accepted 2,000/2,000; wrong answers refused **7,152/7,152** (800 for each of eight integer-answer concepts, 374 for `absmean_quant`, 378 for `softmax`); exit status 0. **The project's earlier record says 6,352/6,352 wrong refused; the shipped harness does not reproduce that figure** (the difference, 800, is exactly one concept's worth of mutants; the cause is not recorded and the harness may have changed after the recorded run). Both numbers are reported; the re-run is the one that can be checked, and in both every wrong answer was refused. The run's output was never saved as a file.
- **Computed status (`docs/appendix/part7/ch5-math/results/STATUS.md`)**: each concept row shows its law id, emitted-row count and `M` (measured). The law and generator columns summarise runs whose ledger and logs are not in this repository; the script that computes the file is not in this repository.
- **Laws (laws stage).** The `math.kb` `check` receipts above record `laws_passing 10/10` at three seeds (PARTIAL: the laws live in an axiom file for the author's law tool, `axioms/mathkb.json`, and its ledger, neither of which is in this repository, so the receipts cannot be re-run). Recorded but **unsupported (not claimed):** 4,000 passed and 0 failed for each law, and the teeth checks in which 8 of 10 laws failed on a broken subject as they must and 2 (`hamming`, `softmax`) were too weak and were strengthened [needs `axioms/mathkb.json` and the law ledger].
- **Recorded but unsupported (not claimed):** every concept's equation equals the definition on 55 to 408 probe rows, with three seeded wrong variants refused (Barrett shift 31, Fibonacci wrong start, softmax without the max shift) [needs the black-box run logs]; the wall passes 10/10 with 80 seeded leaks refused (the 10/10 is the 10-row wall output above; the 80 seeded leaks need the wall's summary output).
- **Not built:** concepts beyond the ten (the other 135 of the index); laws in more language lanes (C, JS); anti-unification over trees.
- **FIELD PREDICTS.** A property that passes its own subject proves little unless a mutated subject fails it (mutation testing, DeMillo, Lipton and Sayward, 1978; property-based testing, QuickCheck, Claessen and Hughes, 2000): predicts that some first-draft laws are too weak. Observed (recorded, unsupported numbers above): 2 of 10. Identifying a concept from its values is the lookup done by the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences (Sloane); learning a library of reusable abstractions is DreamCoder (Ellis et al., PLDI 2021); proof libraries as laws is the Lean mathematical library. These are named as platforms in the planning notes, with no numeric prediction recorded.
